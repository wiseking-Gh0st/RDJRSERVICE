Meet Rohan Fray

Rohan Fray is the proud owner and visionary entrepreneur behind Rohandanjr’s Shopping Services. As an upcoming entrepreneur, Rohan is passionate about providing seamless and dependable shopping solutions to customers both locally and internationally.

With a commitment to quality, trust, and customer satisfaction, Rohan has grown the company into a reputable service offering shopping convenience via plane and ship transport, secure payments through various channels, and a personalized approach to each order.

Rohan’s dedication to excellence is reflected not only in the company’s slogan, “Anything you want, say it & we order it,” but also in the hands-on way he manages every detail, ensuring all customers receive their products on time and in perfect condition.

Looking ahead, Rohan Fray aims to expand the business, innovate new service offerings, and continue empowering customers by making shopping accessible and hassle-free.
